# Language

## Linguistic Guide: Imperial de Tartessa and The People’s Republic of Amerland

This guide outlines the official and vernacular languages of the two primary global powers, centered on the legacy of the **Old Kingdom of Aristia**.

---

### I. Imperial de Tartessa (The Empire)

The Empire operates on a linguistic hierarchy where the "Conqueror’s Tongue" sits atop the "Legacy Tongue" of the mainland.

* **Tartessano (The High Tongue)**
    * **Basis:** Deep Tagalog with heavy Spanish-archaic influence.
    * **Usage:** Official state documents, royal decrees, religious liturgy, and high-court diplomacy.
    * **Speaker Profile:** Royal family (Marleon), high-ranking nobility, and Preacher Monks. 
    * **Aura:** Formal, rhythmic, and honorific-heavy. It is the language of the "Legendary Tartes."

* **Aristiano (The Common/Service Tongue)**
    * **Basis:** "Filipino English." A derivation of Old Aristian heavily influenced by Tartessano syntax.
    * **Usage:** Military logistics, trade, inter-provincial communication, and technical manuals.
    * **Speaker Profile:** The "Specks of Dust"—soldiers like Emilio and June, dockworkers, and mainland citizens.
    * **Aura:** Functional, punchy, and pragmatic. It is the language of the "Mainland" under Imperial rule.

* **Haluan (Informal/The Mix)**
    * **Note:** Not an official language.
    * **Basis:** "Taglish." A fluid blending of Tartessano and Aristiano.
    * **Usage:** Barracks talk, street markets, and high-stress situations (e.g., combat). 
    * **Aura:** Expressive and fast. It signifies a "Mainlander" identity.

---

### II. People’s Republic of Amerland (The Republic)

The Republic defines itself by its preservation of "Old World" sophistication against what they perceive as Imperial barbarism.

* **Standard Aristian (The Registry)**
    * **Basis:** Sophisticated "British" English.
    * **Usage:** All official documents, parliamentary debates, and international trade.
    * **Speaker Profile:** Republican citizens, descendants of Old Aristian refugees, and the academic elite.
    * **Aura:** Precise, grammatically rigid, and literal. It avoids "loanwords" from Tartessano at all costs.

* **Regional Dialects**
    * **Usage:** Within the various principalities and thalassocracies that form the Republic.
    * **Note:** These are often suppressed in official settings in favor of **Standard Aristian** to maintain the "Alliance" identity.

### IV. Practical Implementation for Narrative

To maintain consistency without linguistic fatigue, apply the following structural rules:

1.  **High Tartessano (June's Home Life):** Written in formal, slightly archaic English. Use Latinate words and indirect phrasing.
2.  **Aristiano (The Scrapper Crew):** Written in standard English but with Filipino sentence structures (e.g., placing the verb or adjective first, or using particles).
    * *Example:* "Stuck again, the valve is." or "Check the gauge first, June."
3.  **Standard Aristian (The Republic Enemy):** Written in crisp, highly technical, and literal English. No slang.
4.  **Haluan (The Code-Switch):** Use English for technical actions and Filipino for "Soul Words" (interjections/emotions).
    * *Keywords:* **'pre**, **'di ba**, **sayang**, **sige**, **naku**, **pala**.
